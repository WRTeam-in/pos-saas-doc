Support

Support is a ticket inbox between your business and the platform team. Replies appear in the thread and are emailed to you.

Where to find it: sidebar > Support, or the Support button in the top bar. Open to every team member, with no permission or plan needed. It keeps working even when the subscription is read-only.

Send a request​

  1. Click New request.
  2. Enter a Subject (4 to 120 characters).
  3. Pick a Type: Question, Billing, Something is broken or Feature request.
  4. Describe the problem under How can we help? (5 to 5,000 characters).
  5. Click Send.

You can send up to 10 new requests per hour.

Follow the conversation​

Tabs filter the list: all, Waiting for us, Answered and Closed, each with its count. Search by subject or request number (for example #12). The list shows Subject, Type, Status and Last update. Open requests stay on top.

StatusMeaning
Waiting for usThe team has not answered yet.
AnsweredThe team replied.
ClosedThe request is finished.

Open a request to see the Conversation. Type under Your reply and send with Enter (Shift+Enter for a new line). Any reply puts the request back in front of the team.

Close a request​

Click Close request and confirm. You can reopen it by replying. Once closed, a request cannot take new messages from you. Open a new request instead.
