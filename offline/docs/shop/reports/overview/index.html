Reports Overview

Reports turn your sales, payments, purchases, stock and expenses into totals you can filter by date and branch. Every report can be exported to CSV.

Where to find it: sidebar > Reports. Each report needs its own permission (see below).

The reports​

ReportPermissionQuestion it answers
Sales Reportreports.salesHow much did we sell, and how much was refunded?
Payments Reportreports.salesHow much money came in, and by which method?
Purchases Reportreports.financialHow much did we spend with suppliers?
Profit Reportreports.financialWhat is the gross and net profit?
Inventory Reportreports.inventoryWhat stock do we hold and what is it worth?
Expiry Reportreports.inventory + plan feature Batch and expiry trackingWhich batches have expired or are about to?
Coupons reportreports.financial + plan feature CouponsHow often was each coupon used?
Gift cards reportreports.financial + plan feature Gift cardsWhat did we sell, and what is still open on gift cards?

A menu item only shows if you hold the permission. The Expiry Report is also hidden when your plan does not include batch and expiry tracking. See Roles & Permissions to grant access.

Shared filters​

The Sales, Payments, Purchases and Profit reports use the same filter bar.

FieldWhat it does
From / ToLimits the report to a date range. Leave both empty for all time.
BranchLimits the report to one branch. All branches covers every branch you may access.
ApplyReloads the report with your choices.
  • Dates are read in your business time zone. A shop's "9 October" starts at the shop's midnight, not UTC midnight.
  • If From is after To, the two are swapped and a notice tells you.
  • An invalid date is ignored and a notice names it.
  • You only ever see branches you are assigned to. Asking for a branch you cannot access shows all of your branches instead.

The Inventory and Expiry reports show stock as it is now, so they have no date range, only a Branch filter.

Summary cards and table​

Each report starts with summary cards for the whole filtered period, followed by a table that shows 15 rows per page. The cards cover every matching row, not just the page you are on. The table headings stay in view while you scroll.

Export CSV​

Click Export CSV to download the filtered report. The file contains every matching row, not only the current page, and respects your filters.

ReportFile name
Salessales-report.csv
Paymentspayments-report.csv
Purchasespurchases-report.csv
Profitprofit-report.csv
Inventoryinventory-report.csv
Expiryexpiry-report.csv
note

In read-only mode (an expired subscription) reports and exports still work. See Billing & Plans.
