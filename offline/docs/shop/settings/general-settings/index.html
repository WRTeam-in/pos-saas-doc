General Settings

Settings is where you describe your business and tune how the panel and the POS behave. One page holds most sections as tabs, and a few other pages (payment methods, email, SMS, daily summary) open from the same menu.

Where to find it: sidebar > System > Settings. Requires settings.manage.

How the page works​

  • The section list on the left is grouped: General (Appearance, Business, Brand, Money & tax), Sales (Receipt template, Cashier (POS), Payments, Payment Methods, Loyalty), Messaging (Email settings, Email templates, SMS settings, SMS templates) and Alerts & backups (Daily summary).
  • The tabs on this page share one form. Click Save settings to save every tab at once. A bar shows Unsaved changes and nothing is applied until you save.
  • A red ! badge on a section marks a field that needs attention after a failed save.
  • Loyalty only shows if you hold loyalty.manage. See Loyalty.
  • Sections your plan does not include (for example Loyalty, Gift cards, SMS settings, SMS templates or Daily summary) are hidden from the list. See Billing and plans.

Appearance​

Choose how the admin looks. Changes apply to everyone in the business once you save, and you can preview them first.

FieldWhat it does
Accent colorColour of buttons, links, toggles and charts. Pick a suggested swatch, choose Custom, or type a 6-digit hex value. Reset returns to the built-in palette.
Text on accentColour of labels on accent-filled buttons: Auto, White or Dark. A badge warns when the contrast is Hard to read.
Default themeWhat people see until they choose their own: Light, Dark or System.
FontOne of the curated fonts, used across the whole admin.
Guided tourSwitch for showing the system tour to users.
Setup guideReopen brings back the setup checklist. See Setup Wizard.

Money & tax​

How prices are written and the tax added at checkout.

FieldWhat it does
Regional presetsOne click fills the currency format for US, Euro, UK or India. You can adjust anything afterwards.
CurrencyUSD, EUR, GBP or INR.
SymbolThe currency symbol shown with amounts (up to 6 characters).
Thousands separatorComma (1,234), Dot (1.234), Space (1 234) or None (1234).
Decimal separatorDot (12.50) or Comma (12,50).
Put the symbol before the amountPlaces the symbol before or after the number.
Default tax rate (%)Charged on taxable products at checkout. 0 to 100.
Price typeExclusive (tax added on top) or Inclusive (tax included in the price).
Date format2026-10-07 (year-month-day), 07/10/2026 (day/month/year), 10/07/2026 (month/day/year) or 07 Oct 2026.
Time format24-hour (22:20) or 12-hour (10:20 PM).
warning

Changing the currency does not convert existing product prices. A notice appears after saving with a Review product prices link. Past sales and refunds keep the currency they were made in.

For tax rates and groups beyond the default rate, see Tax Rates & Groups.

Receipt template​

Controls what prints on the customer receipt.

FieldWhat it does
Paper size58 mm thermal roll, 80 mm thermal roll or A4 sheet. Sets width and column layout. On a roll the print page is exactly as long as the receipt, so nothing is cut off and no blank paper is fed. A4 sheet prints an invoice layout with a Billed to box and a list of payments.
Text sizeSmall, Normal (default), Large or Extra large. A sample below the field shows the result.
Text thicknessNormal or Bold (darker). Choose Bold if the printed text looks too light, which is common on thermal printers.
Print logo at the topIncludes your logo.
Print customer name + phoneIncludes the customer.
Print cashier nameIncludes the cashier.
Print SKU under each itemAdds the SKU line.
Print tax breakdownShows tax per rate.
Print sale barcodeAdds a barcode of the sale number.
Print QR codeAdds a QR code of the sale number. Scanning it into a search box, for example when taking a return, finds the sale.
Print batch and expiry under tracked itemsFor batch-tracked products.
Header textUp to 500 characters, printed above the items. Usually shop name, address and tax number.
Footer textUp to 200 characters, printed below the totals. A thank-you note or social handle.
Return policyUp to 500 characters, printed at the very bottom in fine print.

Inventory​

FieldWhat it does
Default low stock thresholdUsed for products with no alert quantity of their own. Products at or below it in the current branch show a Low badge.
Allow stock to go negativeLets sales continue at zero stock. Batch-tracked products never go below zero.
Block sales of expired stockExpired batches are skipped at the till and in transfers. Write-offs and supplier returns can still take them.
Expiry warning (days)Batches expiring within this many days are flagged in the cart and in the Expiry Report. 0 to 365.

Sales​

FieldWhat it does
Allow credit sales (checkout with a balance due)Lets a customer leave with an unpaid balance.
Refund limitThe most a user without refund approval can refund in total on one sale. Empty or 0 means no limit.

Payments​

Lets customers pay by card or UPI at checkout through an online gateway. Choose Stripe, Razorpay, Flutterwave or PayPal from the Gateways list. A coloured dot shows whether it is off, configured, or missing credentials.

FieldWhat it does
EnabledTurns the gateway on. It is active only when the credentials for the selected mode are saved.
ModeLive uses live credentials and takes real payments. Test uses the separate test credentials and moves no real money.
CredentialsStripe: Publishable key, Secret key, Signing secret. Razorpay: Key ID, Key Secret, Webhook secret. Flutterwave: Public key, Secret key, Secret hash. PayPal: Client ID, Client secret, Webhook ID.
Webhook URLCopy it into the gateway's dashboard so payments confirm even if the customer's browser never returns.
  • Secret values are stored encrypted and shown as Saved. Click Replace to change one; leave it blank to keep it.
  • Live and test credentials are kept separately, so you can switch modes without retyping.
  • PayPal only appears at checkout when it supports your currency.
  • A gateway in Test mode is labelled Test mode at checkout.

For cash, card terminal, UPI and other manual methods, see Payment Methods.
