Discount Rules

A discount rule gives an automatic discount at the POS when its conditions are met. No code or manual entry is needed. For a code the cashier enters, use coupons.

Where to find it: Tax & Pricing > Discount Rules. Permissions: discount-rules.view, discount-rules.create, discount-rules.update.

Create a rule​

  1. Click New Discount Rule.
  2. Fill in the fields below.
  3. Click Save Discount Rule.
FieldWhat it does
NameShown in reports and the list. Unique.
TypePercentage (up to 100) or Fixed (a flat amount off).
ValueThe percentage or amount. At least 0.01, two decimals.
ScopeProduct, Variant or Sale.
BranchLimit to one branch, or All branches.
Customer GroupLimit to one customer group, or Any customer.
StatusInactive stops the rule applying, without deleting it.
Start date / End dateOptional. The rule only applies between them. The end must be after the start.
Min. quantityOptional. Quantity of the item in the cart needed (Product and Variant scope).
Min. sale amountOptional. Sale subtotal needed (Sale scope).
Products / VariantsRequired for Product and Variant scope. Search, Select all or Clear all.

How rules are applied​

  • Product and Variant rules discount each matching line. The price they discount is the price list price if one applies, otherwise the product's price. If several rules match a line, only the single best one (largest amount) is used; they do not stack. A rule never takes a line below zero.
  • Sale rules discount the whole basket once the subtotal reaches Min. sale amount. Again the best single rule wins, and the discount is shared across the lines. A fixed amount is capped to the basket.
  • Tax is calculated on the amount after the discount. See Tax rates and groups.
note

A product's own Discounted price (set on the product) is separate from discount rules. It becomes the starting price that rules then discount.
