Feature rollout

Plans decide which yes/no features a business has. Feature rollout lets you override that for selected businesses: a beta, a gift, or a fix.

Where to find it: sidebar, Features. Needs features.manage.

The features​

The list shows each yes/no feature, the plans that include it (Included in plans), and how many businesses are Forced on and Forced off. Limits such as branches or products are not listed here; set those per business under Businesses.

FeatureWhat it controls in a business
Loyalty pointsLoyalty settings and customer points
Batch and expiry trackingBatch and expiry screens and the expiry report
Stock takeStock takes
QuotationsQuotations
Daily owner summaryThe owner summary settings
SMSSMS settings and templates

Roll a feature out​

  1. Open Features and click a feature.
  2. The table shows, for each business, By plan, Override and Effective.
  3. Tick the businesses (up to 500).
  4. Choose Force on, Force off or Follow the plan.
ChoiceResult
Force onThe business has the feature, whatever its plan says
Force offThe business does not have it
Follow the planRemoves the override, so the plan decides again

The change applies at once. Each rollout is one entry in the Audit log.

note

The same override can be set per business on the Manage page, under "Special limits for this business". Rollout is the bulk way to do it.
