# Profile

Your profile holds your own name, photo and password. Everyone in the business can open it.

**Where to find it:** your name or avatar in the top bar > **My Profile**. No special permission needed.

<Screenshot name="shop-profile" alt="My Profile page with member details and change password" />

## Member details

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Profile photo** | Optional. JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 2 MB. You can also remove the current photo. |
| **Name** | Your display name. |
| **Email** | Your login email. Read-only here. |
| **Role** | Your role in this business. Read-only. |
| **Branches** | The branches you are assigned to. Read-only. |

Click **Save changes**. Only an owner or manager can change your role or assigned branches. See [Team](../people/team.md).

## Change password

1. Enter your **Current password**.
2. Enter a **New password** and **Confirm password**. It must differ from the current one and follow the password rules the platform sets.
3. Click **Change password**.

Changing your password signs you out of all your other devices.

:::note
Your profile still saves when the subscription is read-only.
:::
