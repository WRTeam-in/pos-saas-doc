Navigating the Panel

The panel has a sidebar for pages and a header for tools you use everywhere: search, notifications, switching business or branch, language, colours and your account menu.

Sidebar​

  • The Dashboard sits at the top. Everything else is in collapsible groups: Sales, Inventory, Tax, Pricing, Purchasing, Expenses, Branches, People, Reports, Help and System.
  • You only see items your role can open and your plan includes. See the table in the Overview.
  • Use the menu button in the header (Toggle sidebar) to collapse or expand it.
  • The group containing the page you are on stays open.

Header tools​

ToolWhat it does
Search box"Search products, customers, sales..." finds products, customers and sales, and also lists sidebar pages.
SupportOpens the Support page. Open to every member.
Notifications (bell)A count and a list of what needs attention: products low or out of stock, batches expired or expiring soon, and customers with dues older than 30 days. Each links to the matching report or customer list when you have access. View all notifications opens the full page. Not shown on the POS terminal.
Business switchAppears only if your account belongs to more than one business.
Branch switchAppears only if you can work in more than one branch. The panel shows data for the branch you pick.
LanguageAppears when more than one language is active.
Colour palettePick an accent colour (Black, Amethyst, Ocean, Rose). Hidden when the owner fixed an accent colour in Settings.
Account menuShows your name, email, business and branch. Contains Profile, the Theme switch (Light, Dark, System) and Logout.

Quick page search​

Press Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on Mac) to open Search pages.... Type to filter the sidebar pages, use the arrow keys to move, Enter to open and Esc to close.

Banners you may see​

  • Free trial reminder with days left, and a link to Billing (you can dismiss it for the session).
  • Payment overdue (grace period) and subscription not active (read-only) notices.
  • Verify your email, shown to the owner when the platform requires verification.
  • A maintenance notice or announcements set by the platform administrator.

The guided tour​

Select Take a tour at the bottom of the sidebar. The tour walks through the Dashboard, the menu groups and how to add a product, and only shows steps you have access to. Use Next, Back or Skip tour; you can resume later from the same button. The owner can turn the tour off under Settings > Appearance ("Show the system tour to new users").
