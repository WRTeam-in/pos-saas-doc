# Languages

The panel can be shown in any language the platform has switched on. Each person picks their own language from the header.

**Where to find it:** the globe button in the top bar. No permission needed. The button only appears when more than one language is active.

<Screenshot name="shop-language-switcher" alt="Language dropdown in the top bar" />

## Switch your language

1. Click the globe button in the header. It shows the current language code.
2. Under **Select language**, choose a language by its native name.

The page reloads in that language and the choice is remembered for your session. It affects only you, not other team members. The same switcher is on the login pages and public pages.

## Included languages

The application ships with English (`en`), Hindi (`hi`), Arabic (`ar`) and French (`fr`) translation files. Which of them appear in the menu depends on what the platform administrator has enabled.

Languages written right to left, such as Arabic, switch the whole panel to a right-to-left layout.

## Add or edit a language

Adding a language, editing its translations, disabling it, setting the default and marking it right-to-left are done by the platform administrator, not from the shop panel. A new language starts as a copy of English, so nothing shows blank until it is translated.

:::note
Email and SMS templates keep separate wording per language. See [Email Settings](email-settings.md#email-templates).
:::
