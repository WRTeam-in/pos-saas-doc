Plans

A plan is what a business subscribes to: a price, a trial length, and a set of limits and features. You can edit the default plans or add your own.

Where to find it: sidebar, Plans. Viewing needs billing.view; creating and editing need billing.manage.

Default plans​

A fresh install creates four plans in USD. They are only created when no plan exists, so your edits are never overwritten. Treat these values as a starting point.

PlanMonthlyYearlyBranchesTeam membersProducts
Trial (14 trial days, not public)0035Unlimited

Trial includes every yes/no feature. Starter includes only Coupons and Gift cards. Growth includes all except SMS. Pro includes all.

Create or edit a plan​

Click New plan, or Edit on a row.

FieldWhat it does
NameShown to businesses
CodeShort id: lowercase letters, numbers, - and _. Set once; cannot be changed later
CurrencyThe plan's currency. Payments are never converted
Monthly price, Yearly pricePrices in that currency, up to 2 decimals
Trial daysLength of the trial for the trial plan
Sort orderLower numbers are shown first
StatusActive or Archived
Show on the Billing pageWhether businesses can pick this plan themselves

Under What this plan includes:

  • Limits (branches, team members, products): a number, or empty for Unlimited.
  • Yes/no features: tick to include. These are Loyalty points, Batch and expiry tracking, Stock take, Quotations, Daily owner summary, SMS, Coupons and Gift cards.

Changes take effect at once for every business on the plan. Saving writes "Plan created" or "Plan updated" to the Audit log.

The trial plan​

One plan is the trial plan. New sign-ups start on it.

  • It cannot be archived.
  • It needs at least 1 trial day. With 0 days, new sign-ups would be read-only at once.
  • It cannot be bought. Businesses choose a paid plan before or after the trial ends.

What a business can buy​

A business can only start a checkout for a plan that is Active, shown on the Billing page, not the trial plan, and has a price above zero. The gateway must be enabled and accept the plan currency. See Payment gateways.

When a business moves to a different plan, it must be within that plan's limits. Otherwise checkout is refused with a message naming the limits it exceeds. Renewing the same plan is never refused.

Archiving a plan hides it from new purchases. It does not move businesses already on it.

Overrides​

A business can have its own limits or feature values, set on its Manage page or through Feature rollout. An override wins over the plan.
