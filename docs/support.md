## What to include

- Your application version (see the [changelog](changelog.md)) and PHP version. The PHP version is under Settings > System > **System health** in the Super admin panel.
- What you did, what you expected and what happened.
- Any message on screen, and the latest entries in `storage/logs`.
- Screenshots if they help.
