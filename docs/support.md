# Support

Before contacting support, check the [FAQs](faqs.md) and the relevant page of this documentation.

## Contact

| Topic | Who | Contact | When to use |
|-------|-----|---------|-------------|
| ✉️ General | Support Team | [support@wrteam.in](mailto:support@wrteam.in) | Anything else. Replies within 1 working day. |
| 🛠️ Technical | Developer | [vipul.wrteam@gmail.com](mailto:vipul.wrteam@gmail.com) · [Chat on Microsoft Teams](https://teams.live.com/l/invite/FEApP6xzBUkr4Wtbg?v=g1) | Installation, setup, errors and other technical issues. |

## What to include

- Your application version (see the [changelog](changelog.md)) and PHP version. The PHP version is under Settings > System > **System health** in the Super admin panel.
- What you did, what you expected and what happened.
- Any message on screen, and the latest entries in `storage/logs`.
- Screenshots if they help.
