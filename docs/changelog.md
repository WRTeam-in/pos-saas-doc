## Version 1.0.0 - `[DATE]`

First release.

**Shop admin panel and POS**

- POS terminal, sales, refunds, shifts and cash, and quotations.
- Products with variants, categories, brands and units, and label printing.
- Stock levels, batches with expiry, adjustments, transfers and stock takes.
- Tax rates, price lists, customer groups and discount rules.
- Suppliers, purchase orders, reorder suggestions and purchase returns.
- Expenses, branches, customers, loyalty points, team, roles and permissions.
- Reports, an owner summary, backups, data export and an audit log.
- Payment methods, email and SMS settings, and multiple languages.

**Super admin panel**

- Businesses, impersonation and feature rollout.
- Plans, payment gateways (Stripe, Razorpay, PayPal, Flutterwave), invoices and reports.
- Support inbox and announcements.
- Platform settings, legal pages, mail templates, languages and backups.
- Admins, roles and permissions, and an audit log, with two-factor sign-in.

**Platform**

- Public landing and pricing pages, sign-up and onboarding.
- Browser-based installer with purchase code check.
- Scheduler for loyalty, subscriptions, summaries and backups.
